Reading the journey page

This is the end-to-end trail for every email the mailbox has received, from arrival to a delivered (or waiting) JSON payload.

Pipeline
Where the email currently sits: Received → Classified → Extracted → Normalizing → Awaiting review / Complete, or Error if something failed outright.
Outcome — auto-approved
The model's extraction had no validation flags and cleared the confidence bar, so it was approved automatically and queued for delivery — no human touched it.
Outcome — approved
A reviewer checked the extraction (edited it or not) and approved it by hand.
Outcome — awaiting review
Sitting in the human queue: either it didn't clear the auto-approve bar, or it carries a validation flag worth a second look.
Outcome — not a remittance / rejected
The model decided the email isn't a payment notice, or a reviewer rejected it.
Confidence
The model's self-reported confidence in the extraction (0–1), one of the signals behind the auto-approve decision.
Delivery
Whether the approved payload has been POSTed to the backend yet — pending, delivered, or failed (with retries).

Need a filtered view instead? Queue lists only what needs a human; Approved lists everything that's gone through, auto or manual.
